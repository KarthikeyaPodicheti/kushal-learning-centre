# Experiments — Kushal Learning Centre

**Baseline to measure before testing:** page visits → WhatsApp clicks → group joins → Day 1 attendance → Day 3 offer acceptance.

The first two numbers are measurable today; the last two live inside WhatsApp and need manual counting for one cohort.

## Experiment Cards

### E1 — Hero: wound-first vs outcome-first
- **Hypothesis:** "You were never bad at maths" outperforms "Teach Vedic Math from home" on WhatsApp clicks, because it names the internal problem this audience carries.
- **Variant:** B = outcome-led H1, same subhead and CTA.
- **Metric:** WhatsApp click-through rate.
- **Sample:** 400 sessions per variant. **Priority:** high.

### E2 — The proof strip
- **Hypothesis:** Adding "13 women have told their story" above the fold increases scroll depth and clicks, because it converts an abstract promise into evidence.
- **Variant:** B = strip removed.
- **Metric:** scroll to 50% and WhatsApp CTR. **Priority:** medium.

### E3 — Sticky mobile CTA
- **Hypothesis:** The bottom bar lifts mobile conversions by keeping the action one thumb away.
- **Variant:** B = no bar.
- **Metric:** mobile WhatsApp CTR. **Priority:** medium.

### E4 — Demo placement
- **Hypothesis:** Moving the 97 × 96 demo above the struggle section lifts engagement, because proof-before-pain is stronger for a sceptical audience.
- **Variant:** B = demo directly under the hero.
- **Metric:** disclosure open rate (needs a lightweight event), WhatsApp CTR. **Priority:** low.

### E5 — CTA wording
- **Hypothesis:** "Reserve my free seat" beats "Join the free workshop" on clicks.
- **Variant:** B = "Join the free workshop".
- **Metric:** WhatsApp CTR. **Priority:** medium.

### E6 — WhatsApp destination
- **Hypothesis:** A direct chat to Deepika converts better than a group invite, because a group feels like a public commitment.
- **Variant:** B = `wa.me` chat link.
- **Metric:** message sent → seat confirmed. **Priority:** high — but test only after the group link is confirmed permanent.

## Rules

- One test at a time, two weeks or 400 sessions, whichever is longer.
- Never test two things in the same section.
- Never invent a deadline, a seat count, or a testimonial to lift a number.
