# Marketing — Kushal Learning Centre

## Target Market & Avatar

**Niche:** Mothers in India with children in Grades 4–10 who want to earn from home by teaching — and who believe they are "bad at maths."

**PVP Index:**

| Candidate niche | Personal fulfilment | Value to market | Profitability | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mothers of Grade 4–10 children who want to teach | 9 | 9 | 7 | **25** |
| Private-school teachers adding a side income | 6 | 7 | 8 | 21 |
| Direct tuition for children (B2C) | 4 | 6 | 5 | 15 |

**Decision:** own the first niche. The second is addressed in a single line of copy; the third is a different business.

**Avatar — "the woman who was told she couldn't":**
A mother in her early 30s, graduate, stopped working after her second child. She helps with homework until Grade 5, then quietly avoids it; her child's maths marks sting. Three free hours a day, a phone full of school WhatsApp groups, and a husband who is supportive but doesn't quite see why she needs this. She does not want "a course." She wants to stop being the woman who couldn't, and to have her own money. She acts when someone she trusts says the thing she is afraid to say out loud.

**USP (swap-test):** *The only Vedic Math teacher-training built for mothers who were told they were bad at maths.* No competitor's name fits that sentence.

**Primary conversion goal:** join the free 3-day live workshop via the WhatsApp group ("Reserve my free seat"). One action, one destination.

**Secondary:** the three Codex bonus modules (included, not gated) — proof of depth, not a separate opt-in.

## Nurture Sequences

**WhatsApp welcome — 5 messages, sent before Day 1:**

1. **Immediately:** Welcome + what the three days cover + one question ("What subject did you last help your child with?"). Asking first builds reciprocity.
2. **Same evening:** The 97 × 96 trick as a 30-second video. Give the win before asking for attendance.
3. **Day before:** Logistics + "bring a notebook and one fear you want to leave behind."
4. **Morning of Day 1:** 20-minute reminder + "you only need to watch, not perform."
5. **After Day 1:** One recap line + the single question to answer before Day 2.

**Rule:** every message must be useful on its own. Nobody should need to have attended to get value from it.
