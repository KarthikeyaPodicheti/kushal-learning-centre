# Positioning — Kushal Learning Centre

## Brand Script (StoryBrand)

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Character** | A mother with children in Grades 4–10 who wants to earn from home without leaving them |
| **Wants** | To be capable with numbers, to teach, and to have her own income and identity |
| **External problem** | She cannot help with maths homework, has no route to teaching, and every income option needs a degree or a commute |
| **Internal problem** | "I'm not the maths type." Shame, quietly carried since school |
| **Philosophical problem** | A woman should not have to be a topper to be allowed to teach |
| **Villain** | The sentence someone said to her in school — not maths itself |
| **Guide — empathy** | "You were never bad at maths. You were never taught this way." Deepika works with women who arrive convinced they can't |
| **Guide — authority** | Founder of Kushal Learning Centre; Vedic Math trainer; 13 real student stories on camera |
| **The plan** | 1. Join the free workshop on WhatsApp. 2. Attend three live sessions. 3. Leave with a method, a class structure, and a first plan |
| **Direct CTA** | Reserve my free seat (WhatsApp) |
| **Transitional CTA** | Watch the 13 stories / try the 97 × 96 trick |
| **Failure stakes** | Another year of stepping back from homework, another year of asking instead of earning |
| **Success** | Her own students, her own timetable, her name on the door — and a child who thinks his mother is brilliant at maths |

## One-Liner

> Kushal Learning Centre helps mothers who were told they're bad at maths become confident Vedic Math teachers — so they can earn from home, on their own terms.

Passes the cocktail-party test: one problem, one solution, one result.

## Key Messages

**Hero hook (locked):** "You were never bad at maths."
The subhead carries the offer, so the hook is free to do emotional work.

**Sticky versions (concrete, not abstract):**

| Abstract | Sticky |
|---|---|
| "Build confidence in maths" | "97 × 96 in four seconds — the trick you'll use on Day 1" |
| "Learn a teaching method" | "Three live sessions, not three months" |
| "Earn an income" | "Your own students, your own timetable, your name on the door" |
| "Join a community" | "Thirteen women have already told their story on camera" |
| "No experience needed" | "No maths degree. No teaching experience. No fee to attend." |

**Customer story (one, concrete):** the 13 video testimonials are the story — presented as real links, not paraphrased quotes. No written testimonial is invented.

**What we do not claim:** no student counts, no income figures, no ratings, no "limited seats" countdown. The only scarcity is the true one: a small live cohort.

## Tone

Warm, direct, unpatronising. Short sentences. Indian English ("maths", "programme", "tuition"). Never "empower" as a verb without a concrete object. No exclamation marks. No emoji.
