The turn

Vedic Math isn’t harder maths. It’s a different way of seeing numbers — one that rewards the way you already think.

Women who “failed” school maths often take to it faster than the toppers did. There is nothing wrong with your mind. There was something missing from the teaching.

Try the method

97 × 96.
Four seconds. No calculator.

This is one of the techniques you’ll use on Day 1. Children who say they hate maths usually just haven’t been shown this.

Show me how it works
Both numbers are close to 100 100 − 97 = 3  ·  100 − 96 = 4
Multiply the two gaps → last two digits 3 × 4 = 12
Subtract either gap from the other number → first digits 97 − 4 = 93
Put them together 93 | 12 = 9,312

Works for any two numbers just below 100. On Day 1 you’ll learn the versions for numbers above 100, and for three-digit numbers.

The path

A clear route from hesitation to a full classroom of your own.

Six steps. The first three happen inside the workshop. The last three are what you build after it.

  1. 01

    Clarity of niche

    Choose your ground — Grades 4–7 or 8–10 — and own it, instead of being vaguely available to everyone.

  2. 02

    Skill building

    Learn Vedic Math from the ground up, in the order that makes it click rather than the order school taught it.

  3. 03

    Teaching confidence

    Practise standing in front of a class and explaining — until your voice stops shaking and starts leading.

  4. 04

    Student results

    Create visible progress your students can feel and their parents can see. That is what brings the next student.

  5. 05

    Class system & structure

    Run organised, joyful classes — online or at your dining table — with a rhythm that doesn’t eat your whole day.

  6. 06

    Income & growth

    Turn the skill into steady income, then a growing one. Your pace, your terms, your name on the door.

Before you decide

The things you’re quietly wondering.

I was never good at maths. Will I be able to follow?

That is exactly who this workshop is built for. Vedic Math is a different way of seeing numbers, not a harder version of school maths. You start from the beginning — including the parts you were never taught.

Is the 3-day workshop really free?

Yes. There is no fee to attend the three live sessions. After Day 3, Deepika will share the certification programme for anyone who wants to go further. Staying is your choice, not a condition.

Do I need a teaching degree or any experience?

No. Many women arrive having never taught a class. The workshop covers both the maths and the confidence to stand in front of students.

I have children at home. How much time does it take?

Three live sessions, not three months. Dates and timings are shared inside the WhatsApp group so you can arrange the children around them.

Can I do this from my phone?

Yes. The sessions and the group run on WhatsApp, so a phone is enough. A notebook is the only other thing you need.

Will you promise me an income?

No — and be careful of anyone who does. What this gives you is a real skill, a method for teaching it, and a structure for finding your first students. What you build from there depends on the work you put in.
